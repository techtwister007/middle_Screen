## 1. The idea behind the demo

**Most software demos show features. Ours shows the owner their own salon, a few months from now.**

Three ideas make it work:

1. **It's already theirs.** Before the meeting we build a demo copy of the app using their name, logo, Instagram photos, services and prices. The first thing they see is not "our product" but *their* app. People value what already feels like theirs, so it's harder to say no to.
2. **They experience it as a customer, on their own phone.** They scan a QR code, book, and receive real WhatsApp messages on their own phone. Then we flip to the owner view and the booking is already there. One person playing both sides of the business is the moment it clicks.
3. **We fast-forward time.** A "time machine" lets us show what happens 3 weeks after a visit (the refill reminder), after a friend is referred, and after 90 days (the dashboard full of results). The owner sees the future, not a promise.

**What the owner should be able to say after the demo:**
> "My customers get a beautiful app with my name on it, they come back on their own, their friends come, my Instagram fills up, and every month I can see exactly what it earned me. They set it all up for me."

## 2. Sales psychology we design for

| Principle | How the demo uses it |
| --- | --- |
| **Ownership** (people value what they feel they already own) | The demo uses their name, logo and photos. We say "your app", never "our app". |
| **Experience beats explanation** | They hold the phone and do it themselves. We talk less than they tap. |
| **Fear of missing out on money they're already losing** | The opening audit shows money leaking today: unanswered DMs, customers who never returned, few Google reviews. |
| **Concrete numbers, their numbers** | The ROI calculator uses their own visits and average bill, and is deliberately conservative. |
| **One clear proof** | "One refill pays for the whole month" is easy to remember and easy to repeat to a partner. |
| **Peak-end rule** (people remember the best moment and the ending) | The peaks are the nail reveal, the friend's gift and the 90-day dashboard. The ending is "your app can go live tomorrow". |
| **Risk reversal** | A clear guarantee removes the fear of wasting money. |
| **Honest scarcity** | Founding-customer pricing is limited to the first 10 salons, and that limit is real. |
| **Social proof** | Once we have pilots, real results from a salon they know. Before that, the founding customer story itself. |
| **Commitment through small yeses** | Each scene ends with a question they will agree with ("Would that help you?"). |
| **Make it easy to say yes** | Pay the setup fee on the spot with a UPI QR from the same product. |

## 3. Before the meeting: the 60-minute personalisation

Done the evening before, from the platform console's **demo builder**.

### 3.1 Research (15 minutes)

- Instagram: followers, posting frequency, best-performing posts, how many comments ask "price?" or "DM?", whether there's a booking link in bio.
- Google Maps: rating, number of reviews, the latest review date, competitors nearby and their review counts.
- Services and prices: from their Instagram highlights, rate card photo, website or a call.
- Any festival coming up in the next 6 weeks.

### 3.2 Build their demo salon (30 minutes)

- Name, logo and colours (the console suggests colours from the logo).
- Choose the theme that best fits their brand. Prepare a second one to show the switch.
- Services and prices.
- 20–30 of their own public Instagram photos in the gallery, named as designs. **For the private demo only; never published.**
- Their artists' names, if known.
- The seeded customers and 90 days of sample history (section 4.3), clearly marked as sample data.
- A festival campaign pack for the next festival.

### 3.3 Prepare the "money leak" audit (15 minutes)

A one-screen summary built from what we observed:

- "In the last 10 posts, 37 comments asked for price or DM'd. Each is a customer who had to wait."
- "No booking link in your bio."
- "42 Google reviews; the nearest competitor has 180. Most people choose the salon with more reviews."
- "No system that reminds customers when their refill is due."

**Tone:** observations, never criticism. The audit is a gift; we leave it with them whether they buy or not.

## 4. The demo app: what it contains

The demo is the **real product**, running in a special demo mode. Nothing is a slideshow.

### 4.1 Demo mode

- A separate demo salon per prospect, at a private link such as `demo.ourbrand.in/glamnails`.
- Real WhatsApp messages sent only to phone numbers we register for the demo (the owner's and ours), from a demo sender.
- Payments in test mode; the UPI QR for the setup fee at the end is real.
- A small, unobtrusive "Sample data" marker on the owner screens.
- The demo stays live for 7 days after the meeting, then expires with an invitation to go live.

### 4.2 Presenter controls

A hidden panel on the presenter's phone (opened with a long-press or a secret link) with buttons that trigger moments on demand:

| Control | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Fast-forward 3 weeks** | The refill reminder arrives on the owner's phone as a WhatsApp message |
| **Complete visit** | Marks the owner's test booking as done and starts the post-visit journey |
| **Send nail reveal** | The owner receives their "finished nails" reveal with the share kit |
| **Friend accepts invite** | A sample friend opens the gift, books and visits; both rewards appear |
| **Low rating arrives** | The owner gets the alert "Hetal rated 2 stars — call her" |
| **Launch festival pack** | Shows the campaign preview and the WhatsApp message the customers would receive |
| **Jump to day 90** | The owner app fills with 90 days of sample results and the ROI story |
| **Switch theme** | Their app changes to a different look instantly |
| **Switch language** | English, Gujarati, Hindi |
| **Reset** | Back to the start for the next demo |

### 4.3 Seeded sample data

Enough to make every screen feel alive, built from the prospect's own services and prices:

- About 120 sample customers across all groups (new, regular, due, at risk, lapsed, VIP).
- 90 days of visits, bookings by source, referrals, shares, ratings and campaign results.
- Three named customers for storytelling:
  - **Priya** (a regular, Gold tier)
  - **Ananya** (came through a friend's referral)
  - **Hetal** (lapsed, then won back by a message)
- Numbers deliberately modest and labelled as sample. They show how the screens work, not a promise.

### 4.4 Printed and physical props

- A **QR stand** with their salon name, for the moment they scan it.
- A **sample mirror sticker** and **aftercare card** with their branding.
- A printed one-page **"Your salon in 90 days"** summary to leave behind.
- Optional: a small branded **hand-rest prop** for the photo corner. A physical object at the table makes the setup feel real.

### 4.5 Devices

- Presenter phone: controls plus the owner view.
- **The owner's own phone:** the customer experience. This is the most important device in the room.
- Optional tablet: shows the reception kiosk or salon display running on a loop during the conversation.

## 5. The script: 20 minutes, 9 scenes

Each scene has a **purpose**, **what to do**, **what to say** (a guide, not a script to read; speak in the owner's language, whether Gujarati, Hindi or English), and the **question that ends it**. Keep it a conversation: let them tap, ask and wander.

### Scene 0. Opening (2 minutes)

**Purpose:** earn attention by talking about their business, not our product.

**Do:** show the money-leak audit (section 3.3).

**Say:**
> "Before I show you anything, I looked at your Instagram and Google. Your work is beautiful: [mention a specific post]. But I noticed a few places where customers slip away. In your last 10 posts, 37 people asked for the price. How many of those do you think booked?"

**Ends with:** "If customers could see prices and book without waiting for a reply, would that help?"

### Scene 1. "This is your app" (2 minutes)

**Purpose:** ownership.

**Do:** hand them the QR stand with their name on it. They scan it with their own phone. Their app opens: their name, logo, their photos, their prices.

**Say:**
> "This is your app. I built it last night with your photos and your prices. Your customers open it from your Instagram bio, a QR on your mirror or a WhatsApp link. Nothing to install."

**Let them scroll.** Pause. Their reaction here tells you how the rest will go.

**Show the switch:** tap **Switch theme**. "Want it to feel more luxurious?" The look changes instantly. Then **Switch language** to Gujarati or Hindi.

**Ends with:** "Does this feel like your salon?"

### Scene 2. The customer books (3 minutes)

**Purpose:** experience the customer's ease.

**Do:** on their phone:
1. Open the gallery and pick one of their own designs.
2. See price, time and the price builder with add-ons.
3. Optional: **try it on their own hand** with the try-on.
4. **Book this look**, choose a slot, enter their phone number, receive the code on WhatsApp, and confirm.

**Their phone buzzes with a WhatsApp booking confirmation in their salon's name.**

**Say:**
> "That's three taps. No DM, no waiting, no 'what's the price?' And they never had to type their name twice."

**Then flip:** show the owner app on your phone. The booking is already there, with the design they picked.

**Ends with:** "How long does that take today, with DMs?"

### Scene 3. In the chair and the reveal (3 minutes)

**Purpose:** the emotional peak; customers creating content.

**Do:**
1. Tap **Complete visit**. On their phone, "in the chair" mode appears: pre-book the next refill, then the **scratch card**. Let them scratch it themselves.
2. Show the staff **photo corner** on your phone: framing guide, frame, design name, artist credit. Take a photo of your own hand on the branded prop if you have it.
3. Tap **Send nail reveal**. Their phone receives the reveal: photos with their salon's frame, and a ready-made Instagram story and WhatsApp Status with their handle and booking link.

**Say:**
> "Every customer leaves with professional photos of their nails, with your name on them, ready to post. If 10 customers a week share, that's 40 posts a month tagging your salon, and every one has a booking link."

**Ends with:** "How many customers post and tag you today?"

### Scene 4. They come back on their own (2 minutes)

**Purpose:** retention, the core value.

**Do:** tap **Fast-forward 3 weeks**. Their phone receives: "Your refill is due on Thursday. Riya has slots at 4 pm and 6 pm. Book in one tap." They tap it, and it books with the same design and artist.

**Say:**
> "You don't remember to message anyone. The app knows each service's refill cycle and reminds them at the right time. If they don't come, it nudges them again, then the artist sends a personal note. Most salons lose customers simply because nobody reminded them."

**Show briefly:** the automatic journey (reminder, nudge, win-back) as the owner sees it in settings.

**Ends with:** "If even 5 more customers a month came back for a refill, what would that be worth?" Let them answer with a number; you'll use it in the ROI calculator.

### Scene 5. Their customers bring friends (2 minutes)

**Purpose:** growth without ads.

**Do:**
1. On their phone, open **Invite a friend** and send a gift. The WhatsApp share sheet opens with a warm message and a ₹200 first-visit gift.
2. Tap **Friend accepts invite**. Show the gift reveal page the friend sees, the friend's booking, and both rewards appearing.
3. Show **Nail of the Month**: entries, voting, and how every voter becomes a new contact for the salon.

**Say:**
> "Your happiest customers become your marketing. The friend gets a gift; your customer gets a reward only when the friend actually visits. You pay only for real customers."

**Ends with:** "Who are your customers who always bring friends?"

### Scene 6. The owner's morning (3 minutes)

**Purpose:** show that running it takes minutes, not effort.

**Do:** on your phone, show the WhatsApp **daily summary**, then the owner app's **Today** feed:
- "6 customers are due for a refill — send reminders" (tap: done)
- "Tuesday 2–5 pm is empty — launch a slot-filler deal?" (tap: preview: done)
- Tap **Low rating arrives**: "Hetal rated 2 stars — call her". Explain that the problem reaches the owner privately and fast.
- **Launch festival pack:** show the Navratri campaign, what customers receive and the expected cost.

**Say:**
> "Every morning the app tells you what to do, and each thing is one tap. You don't need to learn software. And we set everything up and look after it for you."

**Ends with:** "How long do you spend on WhatsApp and Instagram replies every day?"

### Scene 7. Day 90 (2 minutes)

**Purpose:** show the future, and prove we measure value.

**Do:** tap **Jump to day 90**. The owner app fills with results: bookings by source, repeat rate rising, referrals, shares, Google reviews, revenue by service and artist. Then open the **monthly ROI story**.

**Say:**
> "Every month you get this on WhatsApp: how many bookings came through your app, how many customers came back because of reminders, how many friends came, and the money it brought in, next to what you paid us. These are sample numbers so you can see the screens. Your real numbers will appear here from your first week."

**Ends with:** "Would you like to see this for your salon every month?"

### Scene 8. The ROI calculator, price and close (3 minutes)

Covered in sections 6–8.

## 6. The ROI calculator

A screen in the demo app (presenter controls → **ROI**) that uses the owner's own answers. Always conservative, always their numbers.

**Inputs (ask, don't guess):**

| Input | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Customers per month | 120 |
| Average bill | ₹1,400 |
| Share who come back within 6 weeks today (their estimate) | 40% |
| Monthly plan price | ₹999 |

**Calculation shown on screen:**

| Effect | Assumption (conservative, adjustable) | Extra revenue per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More customers come back (reminders, pre-booking, rewards) | +5 percentage points of returning customers = 6 extra visits | ₹8,400 |
| Friends referred | 2 new customers | ₹2,800 |
| Bookings from people who used to give up waiting for a DM reply | 2 bookings | ₹2,800 |
| **Total** | | **₹14,000** |
| Plan cost | | ₹999 |
| **Return** | | **about 14 times the cost** |

**Then the simplest line:**
> "Forget all of that. One refill at ₹1,200 pays for the whole month. Everything else is profit."

Let the owner change the assumptions themselves. Lower is fine; even at half, the numbers usually work. **If the numbers don't work for this salon, say so.** Losing a deal honestly is better than a cancelled customer in month two.

## 7. Showing the price

Show price only after the value is clear, and show it as a choice, not a bill.

**Order:** the Growth plan first (anchors high), then Studio (the one we recommend), then Starter.

| | Starter | **Studio (recommended)** | Growth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| For | Home studios | Nail studios | Premium salons |
| Monthly | ₹499 | **₹999** | ₹2,499 |
| Setup | ₹999 | **₹2,999** | ₹4,999 |
| Everything you saw today | Core only | **All of it** | All of it + custom design, monthly photoshoot, campaigns run for you |

**Founding customer offer (real, limited to the first 10 salons):**
- Price locked for life.
- Free QR kit, mirror stickers, aftercare cards and photo-corner prop.
- Personal setup: live within 48 hours.
- In return: a testimonial and permission to share real results.

**Guarantee (risk reversal):**
- "If your app isn't live within 48 hours of receiving your menu and photos, your setup fee is refunded."
- "Cancel any month. No lock-in, and your customer data is yours to export."
- Optional, decided at company level: "If the ROI report doesn't show at least your plan's cost in returning bookings by day 60, the next month is free."

**WhatsApp costs:** explained openly. "Messages are charged at Meta's rates, passed through at cost, and shown every month. A typical salon spends ₹X." Use real figures once the provider is chosen.

## 8. The close

**Ask directly:**
> "Shall I make this live for you? Your app is already built. If you give me your final menu and a few more photos today, your customers can use it by [day after tomorrow]."

**Make yes easy:**
- Show the **UPI QR for the setup fee**, from the same product they just saw.
- Collect: final menu, 20 more photos (or permission to use their Instagram ones), artists' names and the WhatsApp number to use.
- Book the 30-minute staff training slot right there.

**If they hesitate:** "What would you need to see to feel sure?" Then handle it with section 9. Never pressure; book a follow-up instead.

**If they say no:** leave the audit and the demo link anyway (section 11). A good experience today is a referral or a yes later.

## 9. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I already use WhatsApp and Instagram, it's fine." | "Keep both. This makes them work harder: Instagram gets a booking link, WhatsApp gets automatic reminders. You saw the reminder arrive on your own WhatsApp." |
| "My customers won't download an app." | "They don't have to. It opens from a link or QR code, like a website. You just did it on your phone." |
| "It's expensive." | Go back to the ROI calculator: "One refill pays for the month." Offer annual prepay: 12 months for the price of 10. |
| "I tried software before; my staff didn't use it." | Show checkout taking seconds. "We train your staff in 30 minutes, and payments through the app record themselves." |
| "EaseSeat / Salos / Basil is cheaper or does billing." | "They're good billing tools. We're not billing software; we bring customers back and bring new ones in, and we run it for you. Many salons can use both." |
| "I don't have time to set it up." | "You don't set it up. We do it in 48 hours. You just send your menu and photos." |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | The guarantee, plus no lock-in. "You'll see the numbers every month. If it isn't earning more than it costs, cancel." |
| "Will my customer data be safe? Is it mine?" | "It's yours. Only your salon sees it, never other salons. You can export it any time." |
| "I'll think about it." | "Of course. What's the one thing you'd want to be sure about?" Then: "Your demo stays live for 7 days. Show it to your team and customers." Book a follow-up date. |
| "Let me ask my partner / husband / family." | "Shall I show them too? I can come back, or send this 3-minute video of your own app." |
| "Messages will annoy my customers." | Show the frequency caps, consent and the one-tap unsubscribe. "At most one offer a week. Reminders only when a refill is actually due." |
| "I'm a small home studio." | The Starter plan: "₹499 a month; one customer coming back pays for it." |

## 10. Shorter and longer versions

| Version | When | Scenes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **3-minute walk-in** | Busy salon, owner between customers | Scene 1 (their app on their phone), Scene 3 (reveal), the "one refill pays for the month" line, leave the demo link |
| **10-minute** | First meeting, limited time | Scenes 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, then price |
| **20-minute (standard)** | Planned meeting | All scenes |
| **40-minute** | Premium salon or several decision-makers | All scenes + staff checkout hands-on + reception kiosk and salon display + platform page editor + custom theme discussion |
| **Group demo** | Nail academy class, association meeting, distributor event | Live on a screen, everyone scans the QR and books; the leaderboard and contest scenes work well with a crowd |

**Remote version:** a video call works too. Send the QR code over WhatsApp; the owner still experiences the customer side on their own phone.

## 11. Leave-behind and follow-up

**Leave behind:**
- The demo link, live for 7 days: "Show your staff and a few customers."
- The money-leak audit as an image on WhatsApp.
- The printed "Your salon in 90 days" page.
- A 3-minute screen recording of *their* app, made automatically from the demo, for partners and family.

**Follow-up sequence (WhatsApp, from a real person):**

| When | Message |
| --- | --- |
| Same day | Thank you + demo link + the audit + "Anything you'd like me to change in your app?" |
| Day 2 | A single change they asked for, already made: "I added your bridal package. Have a look." |
| Day 4 | A relevant story: a festival coming up and the ready pack for their salon |
| Day 6 | "Your demo expires tomorrow. Shall I make it live? Founding price still available." |
| Day 14 | Final, friendly check-in; offer the 3-minute recording for their partner |

**Tracking:** the demo records what the owner did on their own (screens opened, theme switches, ROI changes). If they returned to the demo, that's a signal to call.

## 12. Honesty rules

These protect the brand and stop early cancellations:

- Sample data is always labelled as sample. Never present it as another salon's real results.
- Never invent testimonials. Until we have real ones, say "you'd be one of our founding salons" and make that a privilege.
- ROI assumptions stay conservative and are shown, not hidden. The owner can change them.
- Their Instagram photos are used only in their private demo and are deleted if they don't go ahead.
- WhatsApp costs and what the monthly fee includes are explained before payment.
- No fake countdown timers. Founding pricing ends when the 10 places are filled, and we say how many are left.
- If the product isn't right for a salon, say so.

## 13. Measuring the demo

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Demos to paid setup | At least 1 in 4 |
| Owners who scanned and booked on their own phone | Every demo |
| Owners who returned to the demo link within 7 days | Over 50% |
| Time from demo to go-live | Under 48 hours |
| Most common objection | Logged after each demo; script updated weekly |

## 14. Demo kit checklist

**Product (built as part of the platform):**

- [ ] Demo builder in the platform console (salon from logo, menu, photos in about 30 minutes)
- [ ] Demo mode: sample data, labels, 7-day expiry, test payments, restricted WhatsApp recipients
- [ ] Presenter controls (section 4.2)
- [ ] ROI calculator screen
- [ ] Money-leak audit template
- [ ] Automatic 3-minute recording of the prospect's app
- [ ] Demo analytics (what the owner opened afterwards)

**Physical kit:**

- [ ] QR stand (printed per prospect)
- [ ] Sample mirror sticker and aftercare card
- [ ] Photo-corner hand-rest prop
- [ ] Printed "Your salon in 90 days" page
- [ ] Tablet for kiosk or salon display loop (optional)
- [ ] Charged phone + power bank; offline backup video in case of poor network

**Before each demo:**

- [ ] Research done and audit prepared
- [ ] Demo salon built with their brand, photos, services and prices
- [ ] Second theme and language ready to switch
- [ ] Owner's phone number added to the demo's allowed WhatsApp recipients (with their consent at the start of the meeting)
- [ ] Next festival's campaign pack loaded
- [ ] Presenter controls tested and reset
- [ ] UPI QR for the setup fee ready
