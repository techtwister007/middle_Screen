Signature property page: references and plan

Before building again, here is what I want to learn from four real property websites, the font choice, and the exact order of the page. Nothing here is built yet. Approve or change it, then I build only this one screen.

References

Real pages captured on a phone today. Scroll each row sideways.

The Modern House

London estate agent for design-led homes

  • Photo first, then quiet facts. Title, area and price as plain lines of text. No boxes.
  • Jump links under the header: Floorplan, Map, Brochure.
  • One dark band near the end: "Interested? Let's talk."

Leave: tiny all-caps buttons, no WhatsApp.

Inigo

Sister agency for historic homes

  • A one-line pitch in the serif, before the long description.
  • Photos woven between paragraphs, not dumped in a grid.
  • Warm, quiet ground that lets photos glow.

Leave: long essays; our buyers skim.

Airbnb listing

The benchmark for a property page on a phone

  • Summary line right under the title: 2 bedrooms, 2 beds, 2 baths.
  • Bottom bar: price on the left, one clear action on the right.
  • Show all buttons keep long lists short. A real map for location.

Leave: rating badges, promotional pop-ups.

Lodha, Avalon Juhu

Indian luxury developer

  • Lots of space and one idea per screen.
  • Small serif headings, never shouted.
  • RERA details are always visible, which buyers here expect.

Leave: mood photos without facts; slow to reach price.

Font for headings and price

Last round used Eczar, which is heavy and slightly quirky. The references use a calm book serif. Rasa is that kind of serif, made by an Indian type foundry, with ₹ and Gujarati. Text stays in Hind Vadodara in both.

Recommended: Rasa

  1. Agent name, Share and Save

    Small and quiet. The agent's logo, generated from the name if they have none.

  2. Photo, edge to edge

    Swipe through, with a counter and an "All photos" button that opens a full-screen gallery grouped by room.

  3. Title, area, price, and the agent's one-line pitch

    Plain text in Rasa, like The Modern House. Price with "per sq ft" and Negotiable on one line. The pitch line is written by the AI from the voice note and the agent can edit it.

  4. Jump links: Details, Photos, Area, Cost

    They stay pinned under the top once you scroll, so a buyer can go straight to what they care about.

  5. Details as one ruled list

    BHK and bathrooms, carpet area, floor, facing, furnishing, possession, parking. Amenities follow as a short icon list with "Show all".

  6. Photos between short paragraphs

    Living room, kitchen and balcony, each with one line of description, like Inigo.

  7. Area

    A simple drawn map of the neighbourhood with the home as a circle (privacy kept), the five nearest places, and "How far is your place?".

  8. Cost, then a dark "Talk to Rakesh" band

    EMI and total cost with stamp duty. Then one dark band with the agent's photo, WhatsApp, Call and Book a visit, like the "Interested? Let's talk" band.

  9. Bottom bar: price on the left, WhatsApp on the right

    Like Airbnb. Book a visit moves into the band at step 8 and into WhatsApp replies, so the bar stays simple. Footer: with ❤ from PropPulse.

Please confirm

  1. Heading font: Rasa instead of Eczar. Recommended.
  2. Page order as above, steps 1 to 9.
  3. Bottom bar shows price + WhatsApp only, with Book a visit in the agent band. Or keep WhatsApp + Book a visit side by side.
  4. Background: a quiet off-white, photos and the agent's colour carry the page.
